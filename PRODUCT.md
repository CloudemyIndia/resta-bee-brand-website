## Purpose and audience
RestaBee's public website helps restaurant operators understand the order-to-kitchen-to-bill workflow and request a demo.
## Confirmed constraints
Astro and Firebase Hosting are user-selected. The marketing site is restabee; the POS site is restabee-pos. DNS is on Hostinger and APIs run on Cloud Run. Lead capture will use the existing CRM through a future POST endpoint.
Preserve the supplied logo and recognizable orange brand accent. The user authorizes a complete homepage redesign for clear product understanding and genuine demo enquiries. Visual choices follow [decision 0003](docs/decisions/0003-open-kitchen-homepage.md).
