# 0037 — Natural content depth targets for new product pages

- Date: 2026-11-03
- Status: Accepted
- Scope: New RestaBee marketing pages

## Context

Recent feature routes had useful workflows but left room to answer more of the assigned search intent. Page length should encourage complete coverage without turning a number into a reason to repeat or invent claims.

## Decision

Plan substantive new product pages to exceed 800 visible main-content words. Aim for 1,200 words or more when the page intent and supported product evidence make that depth useful. These are editorial targets, not fixed minimums or ranking formulas. Each section must answer a real reader question or explain a supported workflow. Keep a shorter page when more content would be repetitive, speculative, or outside the search intent.

Report visible main-content word count with the page's SEO completion report. Apply the detailed writing and QA guidance in [content and Astro authoring standards](../content-and-cms-standards.md).

## Rejected

- Treating 800 or 1,200 words as a hard release gate regardless of page intent.
- Padding pages with repeated keyword phrases, generic filler, unsupported claims, or content that belongs on another route.
- Treating word count as a Google ranking threshold.

## Consequences

- The page brief should include enough sections to cover the assigned query cluster and product flow before styling begins.
- The visible-main-content word count is reported as editorial context, not a pass/fail ranking score.
- Existing shipped pages are not automatically expanded; revise them when the user requests it or a separate content review identifies a clear gap.
