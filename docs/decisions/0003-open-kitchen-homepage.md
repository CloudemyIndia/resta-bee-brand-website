# 0003 Open-kitchen homepage
Date: 2026-10-26. Authorized by the user's explicit rejection of the prior homepage and instruction to redesign the entire page with full creative freedom.

## Context
The previous centered software-workspace composition delayed the product illustration, used undersized demonstration text and repeated muted surfaces. The user requested a distinctive design, competitor research, clearer persuasive content, fast responsive behavior and a stronger enquiry path. Humanizer is required for copy.

Research inspected the local rendered homepage and browser captures of [Petpooja](https://www.petpooja.com/), [Restroworks](https://www.restroworks.com/) and [Mews](https://www.mews.com/en). [Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/) supplied content evidence, but its browser view stopped at a verification challenge. Petpooja makes its software visible, Restroworks presents customer logos and awards, and Mews uses large typography with hospitality imagery and product views. Their claims are not evidence about RestaBee. [Creative Bloq's typography discussion](https://www.creativebloq.com/design/fonts-typography/breaking-rules-and-bringing-joy-top-typography-trends-for-2026) informed consideration of expressive, variable typography; it does not establish conversion outcomes.

## Choice
Use an open-kitchen editorial identity: Bricolage Grotesque display, Instrument Sans body, off-white canvas, ink surfaces and vivid orange. Pair an authored restaurant photograph with an interactive, explicitly illustrative order ticket. Show real operational distinctions through concrete examples. Separate service, menu publication, pricing and enquiries into visibly different compositions.

The user delegated design selection. Candidate families considered were restaurant editorial, floor-plan navigation, receipt-led composition, menu typography, dark kitchen-pass signage, food photography and the open-kitchen/photo-and-ticket system. The design seed assigned candidate seven. Ticket-wallet discipline contributes a legible order journey; the crisis-wall and scientific-display alternatives lose restaurant familiarity and product clarity. Use operational status clarity without adopting their decorative controls.

Keep the established Astro/static architecture and public commercial terms. Build the enquiry form as an explicitly labelled email-draft handoff until a CRM endpoint is supplied. Do not show a false submission confirmation. Use examples, exact public prices and contact access for trust. Do not invent testimonials, customers, ratings, usage numbers, response-time promises or savings claims.

## Rejected alternatives
- Further polish of the centered workspace: the user rejected that design.
- A miniature product dashboard as the main visual: details disappear at marketing-page scale.
- Decorative 3D/video/WebGL: unnecessary for the narrative and performance target.
- A pretend lead submission: no server endpoint exists in the supplied configuration.
- A carousel of invented customer evidence: unsupported.

## Consequences
Illustrative photography needs provenance and optimized responsive exports. Workflow states and pricing choices need keyboard support and readable static fallbacks. Enquiry preparation is not proof of enquiry delivery. Real customer evidence and a CRM submission endpoint remain separately scoped work.
