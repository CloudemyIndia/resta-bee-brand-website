# 0019: Separate the close-of-shift features from the operations panel

## Context

The close-of-shift section sat at the bottom of the long dark green operations panel. Its three-column layout left the My Shift screenshot small and visually disconnected from its copy.

## Decision

Place the close-of-shift heading, cash shifts and dues, My Shift screen, reports and team access in a separate light section directly after the operations panel. Use a two-card layout at wide widths and stack the cards at narrow widths; give the My Shift image a larger framed phone treatment.

## Rejected

- Extending the dark green operations panel to include the owner-focused close-of-shift material.
- Keeping the screenshot in a narrow third column between two copy blocks.

## Consequences

The dark operations panel ends after floor, kitchen and billing. The owner-focused content gets a distinct light canvas, a larger screen and a more legible responsive layout.
