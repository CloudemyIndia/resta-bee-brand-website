# 0021: Give close-of-shift cards equal desktop width

## Context

The cash-shift and reports cards sat side by side on desktop at unequal widths, despite each pairing copy with a mobile screen.

## Decision

Use equal-width columns for the Cash shifts & dues and Reports & team access cards on desktop. Keep the existing tablet and phone stacking behavior within each card.

## Rejected

- Giving the cash-shift card more horizontal space than the reports card.

## Consequences

Both owner features receive equal visual weight in the close-of-shift section.
